Finding the right **web developer in Bali** can feel overwhelming. With many freelancers, agencies, and digital nomads offering services, how do you know who’s the right fit for your business? This guide gives practical tips to help Bali-based businesses and entrepreneurs choose a reliable developer — whether you need a simple company profile site or a complex e-commerce store.

## Why work with a local web developer in Bali?

- **Easier communication** — same time zone, faster responses.
- **Understanding of the local market** — many Bali sites need to balance tourism and local business needs.
- **Face-to-face meetings** — possible if you want in-person discussions or workshops.

## Key qualities to look for

- **Technical expertise**: Experience with modern frameworks (Next.js, React) or the stack you prefer (WordPress, Shopify).
- **Relevant portfolio**: Look for projects done for Bali businesses or similar industries (hospitality, villas, cafes, tours).
- **SEO & performance knowledge:** A developer who knows SEO basics, Core Web Vitals, and accessible design will save you time and money later.
- **Clear communication:** They should explain technical choices plainly and provide realistic timelines.
- **Maintenance & support:** Ask what support looks like after launch: updates, backups, bug fixes, and hosting.

## Freelance vs Agency — which is right for you?

**Freelancer**

- Pros: lower cost, direct communication, flexible.
- Cons: limited capacity for very large projects.

**Agency**

- Pros: multiple skills, can handle large or complex projects.
- Cons: higher cost, slower turnaround for small tasks.

**Recommendation:** For most small-to-medium Bali businesses, a **skilled freelancer** offers the best balance of cost, speed, and personal attention.

## Questions to ask before hiring

1. Can I see recent projects and references?
2. What is your process from design to launch?
3. How do you handle SEO and performance optimization?
4. What are your hosting and deployment recommendations?
5. What does support/maintenance cost after launch?
6. Do you provide training for content updates?

## Typical website costs in Bali (estimate)

- **Simple company profile:** IDR 3,000,000 – 5,000,000
- **E-commerce / booking site:** IDR 10,000,000 – 20,000,000+
- **Custom web app:** price varies depending on features

*Tip:* Always ask for a detailed quote with milestones and deliverables.

## How to spot red flags

- No portfolio or low-quality work samples.
- Vague timelines or unrealistic promises (e.g., “site done in 2 days”).
- No contract or unclear payment terms.
- No plan for SEO, performance, or backups.
